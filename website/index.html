Keep your options close

03 / CONNECT

Ask the right questions

Use property-related conversations to contact agents and discuss the details that matter to you.

Take the next step

Request a property viewing and keep track of your arrangements in the app.

05 / LIST

A space for approved agents

Agents submit property details and photos through a listing wizard after account approval.

06 / REVIEW

Moderation built into the flow

Administrator review separates agent approval from property approval. Review does not replace your own property checks.

The right tools for your role

One app.
Different ways to use it.

Property seekers, agents, and administrators have separate workflows and permissions.

For seekers

Find and organize your next possibilities.

  • Browse, search, and filter
  • Save and compare properties
  • Message agents and request viewings

For agents

Bring your properties to the right audience.

  • Verify your email and apply for approval
  • Prepare listings and upload photos
  • Submit listings for moderation

For administrators

Manage the platform’s review process.

  • Review agent applications
  • Approve or reject property submissions
  • Handle flagged reports

A few useful answers

Before you get started.

Can I use NESTA on an iPhone or in my browser?

The current project is a native Android app. This website is its information and download page; an iOS or browser version is not currently offered.

Can any account add a property?

No. Listing submission is for email-verified agents with administrator approval. Submitted properties go through a separate moderation step.

Does approval guarantee a property is safe?

No. Always verify the agent’s authority, inspect the property and documents, and carry out your own checks before making a payment or agreement.

Is the APK ready to download?

Yes. NESTA 1.0.1 Preview is available using the Download for Android button above. The uploaded file matches the APK supplied by the developer; installation and end-to-end app behavior have not been independently verified.
